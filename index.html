About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati (IITG) in my second year. My technical focus lies at the intersection of high-performance systems programming, AI infrastructure, and algorithm design.

I am an avid competitive programmer, using C++ to tackle algorithmic problems involving dynamic programming and bit manipulation. Beyond algorithms, I enjoy building robust low-level systems and the infrastructure around modern AI—from a family of lock-free concurrent queues, to a prefix-aware gateway for LLM inference clusters, to full-stack applications like an AI-powered exam-grading pipeline.
